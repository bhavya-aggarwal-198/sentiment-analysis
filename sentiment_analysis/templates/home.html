A little about this project

Meet SentimentScope.

SentimentScope analyzes a piece of text and estimates whether its tone is positive, neutral, or negative. Choose the standard model or its balanced counterpart, then compare the sentiment breakdown.

✶
3,578,031 sentences used to train the models

Languages & tools

Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Built with Django, scikit-learn, and GSAP.

How it works

Both models use TF-IDF text features and logistic regression to classify negative, neutral, and positive sentiment.

Your text stays yours

Text is analyzed for your request and isn't saved by this app.
